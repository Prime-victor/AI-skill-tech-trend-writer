# Changelog

## 3.0.0 — 2026-10-26

This repository is now **Tech Writer Lite**, the free edition. The firsthand mode, voice guide, site settings, cover images and validation scripts from 2.x continue in **Tech Writer Pro**, available on Agensi.

### Changed

- Skill renamed from `tech-trend-writer` to `tech-writer-lite`. Install it into a folder with the new name.
- The skill covers news and trend articles: breaking-news briefs, roundups and deep-dives.

### Removed

- Firsthand/evergreen mode, references/voice.md, references/cover-images.md, references/output-format.md, references/tables.md, and the firsthand templates.
- scripts/frontmatter_check.py, scripts/table_check.py and scripts/link_checker.py, with their tests.
- The firsthand example post.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-10

- First public version: a news and trend article skill with research, drafting, fact-checking, headline formulas, a link checker and a word-count script.
