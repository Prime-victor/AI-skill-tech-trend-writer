# Headline Formulas

## News and trend (Mode A)

1. **[Company] just did [specific action] — here’s why it matters**
2. **The quiet shift in [technology] nobody’s talking about**
3. **[Number] updates that show where [industry] is heading**
4. **Why [recent development] changes the calculus for [audience]**
5. **[Technology] is moving from [old state] to [new state]**
6. **What [event or launch] tells us about the next phase of [trend]**
7. **The [technology] race is no longer about [old metric]**
8. **Inside [trend]: the trade-off behind [prominent development]**
9. **[Time period] in [topic]: the releases and signals worth watching**
10. **[Company or sector]’s latest move puts pressure on [rival, market, or assumption]**

## Firsthand and evergreen (Mode B)

Use the author's real numbers and project names. If a formula needs a figure the author has not supplied, pick a different formula rather than inventing one.

1. **How I built [project] with [stack or tool]**
2. **What I learned [doing specific thing] for [time period or scale]**
3. **I cut [metric] by [Y]%: here’s what changed**
4. **[Metric] went from [before] to [after]. Here’s how I measured it**
5. **[Number] mistakes I made building [project]**
6. **Why I stopped using [tool or practice] for [task]**
7. **[Project]: a build log**
8. **How [technology] actually works, explained with [concrete example]**
9. **When to use [X] instead of [Y]**
10. **I tried [approach] for [time period]. It didn’t work, and that was useful**
