# Tech-Trend Headline Formulas

1. **[Company] just did [specific action] — here’s why it matters**
2. **The quiet shift in [technology] nobody’s talking about**
3. **[Number] updates that show where [industry] is heading**
4. **Why [recent development] changes the calculus for [audience]**
5. **[Technology] is moving from [old state] to [new state]**
6. **What [event or launch] tells us about the next phase of [trend]**
7. **The [technology] race is no longer about [old metric]**
8. **Inside [trend]: the trade-off behind [prominent development]**
9. **[Time period] in [topic]: the releases and signals worth watching**
10. **[Company or sector]’s latest move puts pressure on [rival, market, or assumption]**
